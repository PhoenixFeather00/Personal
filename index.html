Career Objective

As I start and continue my career as a computer engineer, my number one goal is to make the world a better place and enjoy myself while doing it. This may seem way to vague and generic, so let me be a bit more specific. Making the world a better place doesn't have to be some grand gesture to the world, but rather improving the lives of those living in it. Even if it's as simple as making a few people happy by what I create, that counts in my eyes. I also don't want to be slaving away to acomplish this, I want to love the work I do and feel good to do it. I don't need to have some grandious goal to make a lot of money or invent a groundbreaking piece of technology, all I want to do is make people smile and have a smile on my face while i do it.
